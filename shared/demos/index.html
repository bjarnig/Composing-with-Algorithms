Tools

Rules make material

  • Cellular Automata

    One local rule applied to every cell at once. All 256 elementary rules, the space-time diagram, the rule drawn as its eight neighbourhoods, and the option to read a row as a single cycle of a waveform.

    Composing with Algorithms 01, 24

  • Chaotic Maps

    Nine iterated maps, one and two dimensional, at audio rate or as pitched events, with the attractor, the time series and a bifurcation view of the parameter you are moving.

    Composing with Algorithms 01, 14 · Programming and Music 2, 22

  • Event Generator

    Six distributions and a random walk, applied independently to duration, frequency and amplitude, with scale quantisation and a histogram of what was actually drawn.

    Composing with Algorithms 01, 06, 09
