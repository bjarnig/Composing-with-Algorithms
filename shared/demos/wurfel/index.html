Musikalisches Würfelspiel

Mozart's dice game of 1787. Every bar of the minuet has eleven measures written in advance, and two dice choose one of them. The measures here are written as scale degrees, so the same game can be played in any mode and key. When the two dice show the same number the game can move to a new key.

Two dice give 7 six times as often as 2 or 12, so the pile on the right grows into a peak. The measures in the middle rows of the table are the ones you hear most.
