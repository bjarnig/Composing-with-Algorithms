Musikalisches Würfelspiel

Mozart's dice game of 1787. The piece has sixteen bars, and for every bar there are eleven pre-composed alternatives. Two dice are thrown once per bar, and the sum, two to twelve, selects a row of the table. Nothing is composed at the moment of playing: every possible outcome was written in advance, and the dice only choose among them. The table is the algorithm.

The scheme here is Mozart's. The measures are written for this page, from a fixed harmonic plan, which is the reason any row works in any column.
