Pulsar Synthesis

A pulsaret of duration d followed by silence s, repeated. Period p = d + s. Parameter names follow Pulsar.scd: fund sets the train rate, form the pulsaret rate.

The point of the picture: fund moves the right-hand edge of the period while form moves the edge of the pulsaret, so pitch and timbre are controlled separately. Raise form and the pulsaret shortens, its spectral peak climbs, and the perceived pitch does not move. Lower fund below roughly 20 Hz and the train stops fusing into a tone and is heard as separate grains instead. The duty cycle is capped at 1, so when form falls below fund the pulsaret would be longer than the period and is truncated — the hint says when.
