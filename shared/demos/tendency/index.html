Tendency Mask

After Gottfried Michael Koenig's Project Two. Two limits move over time, and each event takes a pitch drawn uniformly between them at the moment it sounds. Drag the points of the upper and lower limit to draw the mask; the cursor plays it from left to right and starts again. The line under the picture is the same mask written as a pattern.

limits, drag the points events played

A narrow mask is heard as a line, a wide one as a cloud. The density does not change when the mask does; only the range from which each pitch is chosen.
