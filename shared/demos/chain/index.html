A Signal Chain

Four stages in a row: a Gendy, a filter, an oscillator that moves the filter, and a comb delay. Each one is a UGen, and the output of one is the input of the next. The source is Xenakis's dynamic stochastic synthesis: twelve control points whose amplitude and duration each take a bounded random walk, and the waveform is whatever they currently spell.
