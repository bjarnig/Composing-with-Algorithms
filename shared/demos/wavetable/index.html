Wavetable Morphing

A bank of single-cycle tables read by one oscillator. A fractional bufpos crossfades between adjacent tables, exactly as VOsc.ar(bufpos, freq) does in Wavetables.scd; a whole number is plain Osc.ar on one table.

Start with the gain low. The brighter tables have a lot of high partials.

The bank above shows the eight tables; the two outlined in blue are the ones currently being blended. Move bufpos and watch the waveform deform continuously while the spectrum steps between the two harmonic recipes. That is the thing worth noticing: VOsc crossfades the waveforms, so the spectrum you get part-way between two tables is the sum of both, not a smooth glide of each partial. At a whole-number bufpos only one table sounds, which is what plain Osc gives you.
