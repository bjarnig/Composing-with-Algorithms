Event Generator

One percussive sine per event, exactly the \perc SynthDef from the early classes. Each of dur, freq and amp is drawn from its own distribution, as in a Pbind.

Start with the gain low. Short durations plus high amp gets dense quickly.

The upper panel is the resulting texture: every event as a dot, time running left to right, pitch vertically, size from its amplitude. The lower panel is the histogram of the values actually drawn for whichever stream you select, so the shape of the distribution and the shape of the music are visible together. Pwhite fills its range evenly; Pexprand crowds the low end, which is why it suits durations; Pbrown walks in small steps and so draws a melodic line rather than a scatter; Pbeta can be pushed to the edges of the range. Quantising freq to a scale keeps the same distribution but constrains where the values may land.
