Guido's Vowel Table

Guido d'Arezzo's method from the Micrologus (around 1026). The five vowels are laid along the gamut in turn, so each vowel of a text points at three or four pitches. The text decides which vowel comes next; the composer decides among its pitches. The choice rule below stands in for the composer.

With the nearest pitch the line moves by step and sounds like chant. With a random choice the same text gives a different melody every time, from the same table.
